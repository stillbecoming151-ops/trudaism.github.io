Trudaism

Truth as Sacred

"There is only one God" becomes "There is only one Truth."

Many religions began as profound spiritual insights, then grew overgrown across millennia. Trudaism asks: What was the original insight?

  • God = Truth — one reality, forever beyond full grasp
  • We choose, not chosen — anyone commits by valuing truth over comfort
  • Seek forever — the asymptote is the practice itself
