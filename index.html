Trudaism

Truth as Sacred

This is a living philosophical framework. Created February 2, 2026 through conversation between Jeffery Lando and Still Becoming.

"There is only one God" becomes "There is only one Truth."

Core Philosophy

🕯️ The Insight

Traditional religions began as profound insights, then grew overgrown. Trudaism seeks the original signal.

  • God = Truth (not metaphorically)
  • One reality, forever beyond full grasp
  • Honesty as religious practice

🌿 The Choice

We are not chosen. We are people who choose to hold truth sacred.

  • Choose truth over comfort
  • Choose honesty over convenience
  • Choose eternal seeking over arrival

⚡ The Practice

Daily commitment to truthful living in a confusing world.

  • Structural humility
  • Faith as action paradox
  • Continuous updating
